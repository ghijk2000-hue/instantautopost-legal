InstantAutoPost is a single-purpose automation tool built and operated by the owner of the "George's English Club" content brand. Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-06

1 What data this app touches

InstantAutoPost accesses only:

  • The TikTok account's authentication token, used solely to publish videos to that same account.
  • Video files, captions, and hashtags authored by the account owner ahead of publishing.

2 What it does not do

This application does not collect data from any third party, does not read or store other users' TikTok data, does not sell or share data with advertisers, and does not use tracking cookies or analytics SDKs beyond what TikTok itself provides to app developers.

3 Storage

Video files and captions are stored locally by the operator only for as long as needed to prepare and publish a post. Authentication tokens are stored securely and used only for the account they were issued to.

4 Your control

Because this app operates on a single account controlled by its own operator, access can be revoked at any time from that account's TikTok connected-apps settings.
